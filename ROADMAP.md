# Long-Term Vision

Build one of the most practical, well-organized, and community-driven open-source resources for Frontend and Full Stack Engineers.

Success is measured by:

* Helping developers learn effectively.
* Providing production-ready examples.
* Supporting interview preparation.
* Encouraging community contributions.
* Maintaining high-quality technical documentation.
